# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Assume good intent and discuss ideas, not people.
- Respectfully disclose security concerns through the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Keep discussion focused on improving RedReady.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's security reporting channel. Maintainers may remove content, warn participants, or restrict participation when necessary. Reports will be handled promptly and confidentially where possible.

By participating, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct.
